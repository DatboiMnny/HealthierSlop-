Common questions

  • A product isn't found. Tap Add it from the label — snap the ingredients and nutrition panel and HealthierSlop remembers it. You can also add it to Open Food Facts so it works for everyone.
  • The camera won't scan. Allow camera access in Settings › HealthierSlop. Hold the phone 15–20 cm away in good light, or use Photo of barcode.
  • Habits didn't add. Habits add themselves when you open HealthierSlop after their set time — the app can't run in the background. Days marked Off are skipped.
  • My data. Your food log, recipes and settings are saved on this phone only. Deleting the app or clearing Safari's website data removes them.
  • Where do the scores come from? See Sources & methodology.
  • Privacy. No account, no tracking — see the privacy policy.

HealthierSlop gives general information only and is not medical advice.
